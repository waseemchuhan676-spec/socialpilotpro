Official whitelist hierarchy and safety rules prohibiting unverified claims and clickbait.

Strict Prohibited Claim Rules

  • NEVER invent payment amounts (e.g. Rs. 10,500, Rs. 13,500 must come from official notification).
  • NEVER invent payment dates or tell users funds have arrived without official verification.
  • NEVER fabricate registration links or suggest unofficial APK downloads.
  • NO sensational clickbait ("100% payment confirmed!", "secret code", "sab ko milay ga").
  • NO political campaigning — content must remain 100% objective and public-service oriented.

Common Government Scam Patterns

  • SMS from personal 11-digit mobile numbers claiming lottery or BISP grants. (Real is ONLY 8171).
  • Demands for "survey processing fees" or advance deposits on EasyPaisa/JazzCash.
  • Biometric retailers deducting unauthorized commission cuts from women's disbursements.
