Install SocialPilotPro on Android

Experience SocialPilotPro as a standalone mobile app on your phone:

Android (Google Chrome):
  1. Tap the three vertical dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap "Install". The SocialPilot icon will appear directly on your home screen!
iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  1. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) at bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap "Add" in the top right.
